About

okay-guard is written in Scala 3 and Scala.js on okay-effects, a family of algebraic effect libraries.

The guard is a program over its own effects: the wire, the journal, the policy, a person, the clock. Every place it runs only handles those effects:

  • your browser (this studio);
  • a Cloudflare Worker that passes messages between your agent and the page;
  • your machine, on Node or the JVM.

A call waiting for you is the program's continuation, kept as data until you answer.

The policy language is okay-proof's okay-policy. Every decision by a rule is a theorem of its kernel, with a certificate anyone can check again.

Built on okay-effects

okay-effects makes what a program does explicit and controllable:

  • types say what a program may do;
  • handlers say how it is done;
  • interceptors put each action through a point of control, and audit it.

okay-guard is that idea applied to AI agents: every action an agent takes goes through a policy, a person if needed, and a journal.
